Retirement Planning: Secure Your Future

Retirement isn't an age, it's a financial number. Track your progress towards that number with precision.

The Importance of Early Planning

Retirement buying is the biggest financial goal for most Indians. With rising healthcare costs and inflation, relying solely on EPF or PPF is often not enough. You need a mix of high-growth assets like equity and stable assets like debt.

Building the Corpus

To reach your retirement goal, you need consistent investing (SIPs) and the magic of compounding. But how do you know if you are on track?

  • Track Growth: Monitor the CAGR/XIRR of your long-term portfolio.
  • Review Allocation: Ensure you reduce risk as you get closer to retirement.
  • Consolidated View: See your PPF, Mutual Funds, and Stocks in one place.

Tools to Help

Use our SIP Calculator to estimate how much your current investments will grow to. Use the SWP Calculator to plan your post-retirement monthly income.

And most importantly, use Arthavi to keep a hawk-eye on your actual retirement portfolio today.
