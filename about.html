Our Mission: Clarity for Indian Investors

Building the gold standard for unbiased, ad-free portfolio tracking in India.

The Problem

I was tired of juggling different apps to see my total investment returns. I was juggling multiple apps and wanted a better, privacy-first way to track my portfolio without linking demat accounts.

The Journey

I planned and built this entire app in 1 month to solve my own problem.

The Solution

A unified dashboard for Indian Mutual Funds and NSE/BSE stocks that focuses on privacy and manual tracking.

Who We Are

Arthavi was founded on a simple yet powerful premise: Indian retail investors deserve better data. In a financial landscape dominated by broker-owned platforms and ad-heavy free tools, clarity is often the first casualty. We set out to build the best portfolio analytics app for Indian investors—one that serves the user, not the intermediary.

We are a team of engineers and finance enthusiasts who believe that net worth tracking should be private, accurate, and completely devoid of conflicts of interest.

Why We Built Arthavi

Most "free" portfolio trackers in India are actually lead-generation engines. They sell your data to lenders, push "recommended" regular mutual funds that pay commissions, or clutter your screen with ads. We rejected that model entirely.

  • No Commissions: We made a conscious choice not to sell mutual funds or stocks. This ensures our portfolio analytics remain 100% unbiased.
  • Privacy First: Your financial data is sensitive. We architected Arthavi to ensure no third-party ever gets access to your portfolio.
  • Indian Context: We built specific features for the Indian market, like CAS PDF parsing (for CAMS/KFintech), XIRR Validation, and support for NSE/BSE stocks.

What Arthavi Is & Is Not

To maintain complete transparency with our users, clarifying our role is essential:

  • We are NOT a Broker: We do not execute trades, open demat accounts, or hold your money. We are a pure portfolio tracker/analytics tool.
  • We are NOT an Investment Advisor: We provide data visualization and performance metrics (like XIRR and allocation), not buy/sell recommendations.

Our Commitment to Privacy

We are committed to maintaining a clean, ad-free environment forever. Our business model relies on premium analytics features, not on selling your attention or your data to the highest bidder. Learn more about our data practices on our Security page and Privacy Policy.
