Security at Arthavi

Bank-grade protection for your peace of mind.

We understand that using a portfolio tracker in India requires immense trust. Security is not an add-on features for us; it is the foundation of our platform. We adhere to a "Security by Design" philosophy to protect your net worth data.

Read-Only Architecture (No Risk of Funds)

The most robust security feature of Arthavi is what it cannot do. As a dedicated analytics platform:

  • No Withdrawals: Arthavi has zero capability to initiate transfers or move funds out of your bank or brokerage accounts.
  • No Trades: We cannot sell your stocks or redeem your mutual funds. Even in the theoretical event of an account compromise, your actual assets remain safe with your broker/depository (NSDL/CDSL).

End-to-End Encryption

We use state-of-the-art encryption standards to ensure your data stays private:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data exchanged between your browser/app and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2/1.3 (Transport Layer Security), the same standard used by leading Indian banks.
  • Encryption at Rest: Your sensitive portfolio data (holdings, values, goals) is stored in databases protected by AES-256 encryption.

Privacy & Data Protection

Your financial footprint is yours alone.

  • No Data Sharing: We do not sell your data to lenders, credit card companies, or ad networks.
  • Privacy Mode: Our dashboard features a "Privacy Mode" that lets you blur monetary values with one click—perfect for checking your stock portfolio performance in public spaces.

Authentication & Compliance

We employ modern authentication safeguards to prevent unauthorized access:

  • Secure OAuth integration (Google Login) to avoid storing passwords directly.
  • Strict session management to auto-logout on shared devices.
  • Compliance with digital data privacy best practices.

Data Deletion Rights

You have full control. You can permanently delete your Arthavi account and all associated portfolio data instantly from the settings menu. This action is irreversible.

Note: While we employ rigorous security measures, no system is 100% immune to all threats. We practice responsible disclosure and continuously monitor for vulnerabilities to keep Arthavi the secure choice for Indian investors.
