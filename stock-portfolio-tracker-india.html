Manual Tracking vs Broker Lock-in

Most stock trackers require you to login with your broker credentials. This creates security risks and locks you into their ecosystem. Arthavi offers a manual stock portfolio tracker for India where you are in full control.

  • Control: You enter the quantity and average price.
  • Privacy: We don't know your broker ID or password.
  • Flexibility: Track stocks held across multiple family accounts in one view.

Real-Time Price Updates

We fetch end-of-day and live prices for thousands of stocks listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). Your portfolio value updates automatically, giving you a clear picture of your daily P/L.

Read-Only Safety

Since Arthavi is not connected to your broker, it is physically impossible for trades to be executed through our platform. This read-only architecture is the safest way to track wealth.
