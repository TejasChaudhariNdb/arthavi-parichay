Why "Free" Apps Are Actually Expensive

If an app is free and backed by VC money, you are the product. The business model is simple:

  3. Sell "leads" to banks for loans and insurance.

This distracts you from your financial goals and exposes you to privacy risks.

The Rise of Clean Investing
