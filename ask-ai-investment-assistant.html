Ask AI: Your Personal Investment Assistant for Indian Portfolios

Chat with your wealth. Get instant answers on XIRR, allocation, and more.

Investment dashboards are great. They give you charts, tables, and percentages. But sometimes, you don't want to filter through ten different columns to find one simple answer.

Sometimes, you just want to ask: "How much have I invested in HDFC Bank?" or "What is my XIRR for the last 5 years?"

That is why we built Ask AI.

Ask AI is an intelligent assistant embedded directly into Arthavi. It has access to your portfolio data (locally and securely) and can answer complex questions in plain English.

Why Chat with Your Portfolio?

Most investors have questions that standard dashboards can't easily answer without custom reports.

1. Instant Specifics

Instead of calculating manually, just ask: "Show me all my mutual funds with negative returns." The AI instantly filters your data and presents the list.

2. Asset Allocation Checks

Wondering if you are overweight on a sector? Ask: "What percentage of my portfolio is in Banking & Finance?" You get an instant percentage breakdown.

3. Performance Comparisons

Context matters. Ask: "How is my portfolio performing compared to Nifty 50 this year?" The AI pulls the benchmark data and compares it with your actual XIRR.

Try Asking This:

  • "What is my current net worth?"
  • "List my top 5 performing stocks."
  • "How much dividend did I receive last year?"
  • "Analyze my debt vs equity ratio."

Privacy First, AI Second

We know what you are thinking: "Is my financial data being sent to ChatGPT?"

Absolutely not.

Arthavi remains a privacy-first platform. Here is how Ask AI works:

  • Anonymized Context: When you ask a question, we only process the metadata required to answer it. We do not feed your entire transaction history into public LLM training sets.
  • Read-Only Access: Just like the rest of Arthavi, the AI has read-only access. It cannot execute trades, redeem funds, or change your password.
  • You Control It: You can choose to enable or disable AI features at any time.

Who Is This For?

Ask AI is for the modern Indian investor who values time. Whether you use Arthavi as a mutual fund tracker or for your direct equity portfolio, the AI assistant simplifies the way you interact with your wealth.

No more Excel formulas. No more mental math. Just ask, and get the answer.
