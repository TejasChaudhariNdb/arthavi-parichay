Why Indian Investors Need a Portfolio-Aware AI

Most Indian investors manage their money across multiple platforms — SIPs on Groww, direct equity on Zerodha, and NPS through their employer. When it comes to understanding the full picture, they're left manually calculating XIRR in Excel or guessing if they're over-diversified.

Arthavi AI changes that. Unlike generic AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini) that know nothing about your portfolio, Arthavi AI has real-time access to your actual holdings through secure, read-only API calls. When you ask "What is my XIRR for the last 5 years?", it doesn't guess — it calculates using your transaction data.

Portfolio Overlap: A Problem Most Investors Don't Know They Have

A very common mistake among SIP investors in India is holding multiple large-cap mutual funds that track the same Nifty 100 constituents. For example, Mirae Asset Large Cap and Axis Bluechip Fund might have 70%+ stock overlap, meaning you're effectively paying two expense ratios for the same exposure.

Arthavi AI can detect this automatically. You just need to ask: "Do my mutual funds overlap?" and it analyzes the schemes' category, AMC, and known index tracking to flag redundancy.

Sector Concentration: The Hidden Portfolio Risk

Many Indian investors are unknowingly overweight in Banking & Finance — both through direct stocks (HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank) and through their mutual funds (which themselves allocate heavily to financials). Arthavi AI can compute your total sector exposure across both asset classes and flag if you're concentrated beyond healthy levels.

Benchmark Comparison: Are You Actually Beating the Market?

XIRR alone doesn't tell the full story. If your portfolio returned 12% XIRR but Nifty 50 returned 15% in the same period, you're actually underperforming. Arthavi AI fetches live benchmark data and compares it with your calculated XIRR — so you know exactly where you stand.
