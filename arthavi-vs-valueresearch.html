Arthavi vs Value Research Online: Which is Better for Indian Mutual Fund Investors? (2026)

Value Research Online (VRO) has been India's most trusted mutual fund research platform for over two decades. Arthavi is a modern portfolio tracker built for the same investor — but for a different stage of the journey. Here's how they compare.

Fund Research vs Portfolio Tracking: The Core Distinction

Value Research Online is fundamentally a fund research tool. Its star ratings, category rankings, portfolio overlap checker, and fund manager history are indispensable when you're deciding which fund to invest in. VRO has built enormous trust over decades by providing unbiased, data-rich fund analysis.

Arthavi is a portfolio tracking and analytics tool. Once you've chosen your funds and invested, Arthavi takes over — tracking what you own, calculating true XIRR from your actual transactions, risk-scoring your portfolio, and delivering AI-powered insights about your specific holdings.

These are complementary, not competing. Many serious investors use VRO to pick funds and Arthavi to track them — getting the best of both worlds.

Where Value Research Falls Short as a Tracker

VRO's portfolio tracker exists as a secondary feature — it was built for research, not tracking. Its limitations are significant for active portfolio management:

  • Manual transaction entry: VRO's portfolio requires you to enter every SIP transaction by hand. Miss a month and your returns are inaccurate.
  • No CAS import: VRO does not support automatic import from CAMS or KFintech. Arthavi imports your entire history from your investment statement in seconds.
  • No AI layer: VRO offers static fund data. Arthavi lets you ask your portfolio questions: "Am I over-exposed to mid-caps?" or "What is my sectoral concentration?"
  • No Portfolio Health Score: VRO rates funds, not your portfolio as a whole. Arthavi combines your holdings into a single 0–100 health score with diversification and risk subscores.
  • Desktop-primary UX: VRO's interface is designed for desktop research. Arthavi is mobile-first with biometric lock, Privacy Mode, and haptic-enabled Smart Insights.

What Value Research Does Well — And Where It Stops

VRO is irreplaceable for one thing: deciding which fund to buy. Its rating consistency, portfolio overlap tool, and category comparison are industry-standard. But the moment you've invested, VRO's usefulness drops sharply. It has no CAS import, its portfolio tracker requires full manual data entry, and it has no AI analysis layer whatsoever.

Most investors who rely on VRO for tracking are unknowingly working with incomplete or stale data — because keeping a manually-entered portfolio current across dozens of SIP transactions requires discipline most people don't maintain.

Arthavi Is the Tracking Layer Every VRO User Is Missing

Arthavi handles everything VRO doesn't. Import your CAS and your complete transaction history loads instantly — no manual entry. Your XIRR is calculated correctly from the authoritative CAMS/KFintech data, not from memory-based entries. Your Portfolio Health Score, Smart Insights, and Ask AI give you ongoing intelligence that static fund ratings can never provide.

The right workflow for a serious Indian mutual fund investor is clear: use Value Research to choose funds, and use Arthavi to track them. Not as equals — but as a research tool and an analytics platform that together give you the complete picture from fund selection through to real wealth measurement.

The Optimal Investor Workflow

The highest-value combination for a serious Indian mutual fund investor:

  1. Use Value Research Online to shortlist funds based on consistency, risk-adjusted returns, and fund manager track record.
  2. Invest through your preferred platform (Zerodha Coin, CAMS, or directly).
  3. Import your CAS into Arthavi each month to track real XIRR, verify health scores, and ask AI to flag any concerns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Value Research good for portfolio tracking?

VRO is excellent for fund research and ratings. Its portfolio tracking feature is basic — requiring manual entry with no CAS import, no XIRR from actual cash flows, and no AI analytics. Arthavi is specifically built for tracking performance after you've invested.

What is the difference between Arthavi and Value Research?

VRO helps you choose funds. Arthavi tracks how your chosen funds are actually performing with real XIRR, risk scoring, and AI insights. They serve different stages of the investing process.

Is Value Research Online free?

VRO has a free tier and a paid premium subscription for full fund research. Arthavi is fully free for all portfolio tracking, XIRR calculation, health scoring, and AI features.

Can I use both Value Research and Arthavi?

Yes — and it's the recommended approach. Use VRO for fund research and selection. Use Arthavi for ongoing performance tracking and portfolio analytics.

Does Value Research calculate XIRR?

VRO's portfolio tracker returns are based on manually entered transactions and may not compute full cash-flow XIRR. Arthavi computes the correct XIRR automatically from your CAS — the authoritative record from CAMS or KFintech.
