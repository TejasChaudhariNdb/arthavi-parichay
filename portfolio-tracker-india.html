The Problem: Fragmented Investments

For the modern Indian investor, wealth is scattered. You might have mutual funds on Zerodha Coin or Groww, stocks on Kite or Upstox, and older investments sitting in physical folios.

The result? You never actually know your true Net Worth or real XIRR. Most "free" trackers solve this by asking for your email password or selling your data to loan agents. That's a compromise you shouldn't have to make.

The Solution: A Unified, Secure Dashboard

Arthavi is the portfolio tracker India has been waiting for. We bring everything together without reading your emails or selling your data.

Mutual Funds via CAS

Simply upload your CAMS/KFintech CAS statement. We parse it locally to reconstruct your entire transaction history and calculate accurate XIRR.

Stock Portfolio Tracking

Add your equity holdings manually or via bulk import. We fetch real-time prices from NSE/BSE to keep your portfolio value current.
