The Problem: Fragmented Investments in India

For the modern Indian investor, wealth is scattered. You might have SIPs running on Groww, long-term stocks on Zerodha, recent trades on Angel One, Aditya Birla Money, or Upstox, and older mutual funds sitting in physical folios.

The result? You never actually know your true Net Worth or real XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return). Tracking across 4 different platforms makes it impossible to see your true asset allocation or rebalance effectively.

How to Track Your Investment Portfolio in India

Ready to get organized? Tracking your portfolio doesn't have to be a weekend chore. Here is the best way to consolidate your wealth.

  1. Track all investments in one place: Bring your Zerodha, Angel One, Groww, and Upstox investments into a single unified dashboard.
  2. Use XIRR instead of absolute returns: If you are doing an SIP, absolute returns are misleading. You must use XIRR to know the actual annualized profit of your cash flows. Learn what XIRR is here.
  3. Monitor asset allocation: Are you over-exposed to risky small-caps? Keep a tight watch on your equity vs. debt split.
  4. Rebalance regularly: Lock in profits and shift investments to maintain your target risk profile.

If you want to track investments for multiple people, check out our guide on how to track family investment portfolios in one account.

See the Difference: Real Portfolio Scenario

Imagine you invested ₹10,000 monthly in a mutual fund SIP for 3 years, and made a one-time ₹50,000 investment in a stock.

Expected Output:

  • Total Invested: ₹4,10,000
  • Because of the staggered SIP investments, your Absolute Return might look like a modest 15%.
  • But your XIRR (true annualized return) could actually be 24% because the latest SIPs haven't been in the market long!

Without a tracker that understands dates and cashflows properly, you might make the wrong decision to sell a well-performing asset.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a portfolio tracker?

A portfolio tracker is a tool that allows you to monitor all your financial investments—such as stocks, mutual funds, EPF, and bonds—in one place. It helps you track your overall net worth and calculate true returns (XIRR).

Which is best portfolio tracker in India?

The best portfolio tracker depends on your privacy preferences. Arthavi is built for Indian investors focusing purely on accurate stock and mutual fund tracking without ad spam. Other alternatives include INDmoney and Groww.

How to track mutual funds and stocks together?

Upload your consolidated CAS statement for mutual funds and sync or add your stock holdings from brokers like Zerodha or Angel One to a unified tracker to view everything on a single dashboard.

Is Arthavi free?

What tools help track my family's entire financial portfolio across all investments?

What sip tools help track portfolio performance across multiple funds?
