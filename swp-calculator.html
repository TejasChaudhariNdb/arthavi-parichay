What is an SWP Return Calculator?

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) allows you to withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund investments at regular intervals. It is an excellent tool for retirees or anyone seeking a steady source of passive income. An SWP return calculator specifically helps you visualize a critical tension: As you pull money *out*, the remaining money continues to generate returns *in* the market. Will your money run out, or will it grow forever?

Understanding SWP Intent: Will Your Corpus Deplete?

The core intent behind an online swp calculator is determining longevity. The secret to a never-ending SWP is ensuring your withdrawal rate is lower than your expected return rate.

  • Scenario 1 (Depletion): You have ₹50 Lakhs. You withdraw ₹50,000/month (which is ₹6L/year or 12%). If the market only returns 8%, your corpus is shrinking every month. Eventually, it will hit zero.
  • Scenario 2 (Infinite Growth): You have ₹50 Lakhs. You withdraw ₹25,000/month (₹3L/year or 6%). If your fund returns 10%, your returns outpace your withdrawals. Your final corpus after 10 years will actually be *higher* than ₹50 Lakhs!

Whether you're looking for an sbi swp calculator or an hdfc swp calculator, use the tool above to balance these two levers.

Why use SWP instead of FD?

  • Regular Income with Growth: Unlike Fixed Deposits where your principal stagnates, a mutual fund SWP allows your underlying principal to beat inflation.
  • Tax Efficiency: When you execute an SWP, you are redeeming mutual fund units. Only the *gains* on those specific units are taxed (as Capital Gains), not the principal. FD interest is taxed aggressively at your slab rate.
  • Rupee Cost Averaging (Reverse): You redeem fewer units when the market NAV is high, and more units when the NAV is low, automatically optimizing your exits.
