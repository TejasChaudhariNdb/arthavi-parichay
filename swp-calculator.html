What is an SWP Calculator?

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) allows you to withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund investments at regular intervals. It is an excellent tool for retirees looking for a steady source of income. The SWP Calculator helps you visualize how your corpus gets affected by withdrawals over time, assuming a certain rate of return.

Why use SWP?

  • Regular Income: Create your own "pension" from your investments.
  • Tax Efficiency: SWP is often more tax-efficient than traditional dividend plans or fixed deposit interest.
  • Rupee Cost Averaging (Reverse): You redeem units when NAV is high and fewer units when NAV is low, but the amount remains constant.
