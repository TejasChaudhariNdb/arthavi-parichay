The Best Way to Track Mutual Funds and Stocks Together in India

Most Indian investors hold mutual funds across HDFC, SBI, Mirae, and Parag Parikh — and stocks across Zerodha, Groww, or Angel One — but don't have a single place to see their true net worth. Arthavi solves this by letting you import your CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) from official RTAs like CAMS or KFintech to get all mutual fund data, and manually add your equity holdings from any broker securely.

Unlike apps like Groww or Kuvera that push you to transact through them, Arthavi is a privacy-first, read-only analytics platform. You get accurate XIRR calculations based on actual cash flows (SIPs, lumpsum, redemptions) following standard compounding formulas, AMC-wise allocation charts, and portfolio overlap analysis — all without sharing your sensitive broker credentials. Your data protection aligns with our core mission of providing secure, uncompromised financial tracking.

Arthavi's Ask AI feature is purpose-built for Indian investors. You can ask it: "Do my mutual funds overlap with each other?", "Is my portfolio too concentrated in banking stocks?", or "How has my XIRR compared to Nifty 50 over 3 years?" — and get real, data-backed answers in seconds. This is not a generic language model; it operates with read-only access exclusively on the portfolio data you securely provide.

Our platform tracks live equity data conforming strictly to authorized streams sourced from the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) indexes, ensuring precision and reliability for every tracked asset.
