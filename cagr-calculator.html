What is a CAGR Calculator?

A CAGR calculator is a financial tool that calculates the Compound Annual Growth Rate. Unlike absolute returns which only show you the total percentage gained, CAGR smooths out the volatility and shows you exactly what your investment grew by, on average, every single year.

How to Calculate CAGR

The formula for CAGR is:

CAGR = [(Ending Value / Beginning Value) ^ (1 / Number of Years)] - 1

Doing this manually can be tedious, which is why an online CAGR calculator is necessary for quick projections.

CAGR vs XIRR

Many investors confuse CAGR with XIRR. You should only use a CAGR calculator if you made a one-time lump-sum investment (like putting ₹1,00,000 into a fixed deposit or buying a plot of land). If you are doing a monthly Mutual Fund SIP, you must use an XIRR Calculator because the timing of your varied cash flows completely changes the math.
