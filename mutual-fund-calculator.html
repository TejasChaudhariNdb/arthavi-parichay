What is a Mutual Fund Calculator?

This Mutual Fund Calculator is designed to help you estimate the potential returns on a lumpsum investment. If you have a one-time surplus amount to invest, this tool shows you how compounding can grow your wealth over time.

Lumpsum vs SIP

While SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is about investing small amounts regularly, Lumpsum investing is about investing a large chunk of money at once. Lumpsum investments are ideal when you have a surplus (like a bonus or windfall) and want to lock it into the market for long-term growth.
