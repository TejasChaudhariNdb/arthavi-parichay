Why You Need an AI Portfolio Analyzer

Building a stock and mutual fund portfolio is only half the battle; maintaining it is where most investors struggle. Standard tracker apps show you the current value and plain returns (XIRR), but they do not provide context. Without context, an investor may hold 10 different mutual funds, thinking they are diversified, while internally, 70% of those funds are heavily invested in the same Top 10 large-cap stocks.

The Arthavi AI Portfolio Analyzer scans your entire portfolio — combining your mutual fund statements and your direct stocks — and presents a unified risk profile. By continuously checking exposure and overlaps, it acts like a vigilant co-pilot that flags risks before market downturns expose them.
